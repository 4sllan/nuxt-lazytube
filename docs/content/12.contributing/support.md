## Why Donate?

Your donation helps to:

- **Keep the project active** - Time dedicated to development and maintenance
- **Add new features** - Implementation of features requested by the community
- **Improve quality** - Tests, bug fixes, and optimizations
- **Documentation** - Keep documentation updated and complete

## Suggested Amounts

- **$2.00** - A symbolic contribution
- **$4.00** - Helps cover basic costs
- **$10.00** - Significant contribution to the project
- **$20.00+** - Great support for development

Any amount is welcome and greatly appreciated! 💜

## Other Ways to Support

You can also support the project without donating money:

- **Star on GitHub** - Give a star to the repository to increase visibility
- **Contribute code** - Make pull requests with improvements and fixes
- **Report bugs** - Help identify and fix issues
- **Suggest features** - Share ideas for improvements
- **Spread the word** - Tell other developers about nuxt-lazytube

## Thank You

Thank you very much for considering supporting nuxt-lazytube! Your contribution makes a difference and helps keep this project free and open source for the entire community.
